Help and Support

Welcome to the Sholynk Help and Support page. We're here to assist you with any questions or issues you may have. Browse through the topics below or contact us directly for further assistance.

How do I subscribe to the newsletter?

To subscribe, enter your name and email address in the subscription form located in the footer of any page, then click the "Subscribe" button.

How can I submit an article or news tip?

Please contact us via the Contact page with your article or news tip details. We review submissions and will get back to you if interested.

What topics does Sholynk cover?

Sholynk focuses on AI, Web3, emerging technologies, and trends shaping the future of decentralized systems.

How do I report a technical issue with the website?

If you encounter any technical problems, please email our support team at support@sholynk.com with a detailed description of the issue.

Can I advertise on Sholynk?

Yes, we offer advertising opportunities. Please reach out via the Contact page for more information.
